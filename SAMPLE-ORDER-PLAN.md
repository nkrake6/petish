## Before payment

Confirm actual stock, supplier product/variation mappings, final supplier product
cost and freight, shipping service, destination coverage and delivery estimate.
Check whether at least two independent suppliers are represented; five product
lines alone do not establish a multi-supplier test. Record missing estimates as
unknown. Supplier freight variance is borne by Petish.

## Follow through to delivery

Verify payment and tax totals; WooCommerce stock changes; correct recipient,
products and quantities in DSers; supplier payment; separate/combined dispatches;
tracking returned to WooCommerce; customer notices; delivery time and condition;
materials/dimensions/finish; and final payout/profit after freight and fees.
Keep real order references, addresses, payment records and customer information
in the private order system, not this repository.

A separate single Blue Cleaning Brush order can test cancellation/refund before
supplier payment. A supported non-AUD checkout currency adds currency handling
to that test. Agree its budget first. Declined payments remain staging tests;
another destination requires verified shipping coverage before purchase.
